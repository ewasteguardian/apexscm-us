What we do, and where.

01 · In the United States

Electronics: collected, processed, refined.

We are an established operator in the collection, secure processing and refining of end-of-life electronics. Our lifecycle model connects data destruction and refurbishment with material recovery and reintegration, under chain-of-custody control and documented outcomes.

  • Secure collection and certified data destruction
  • Processing, refurbishment and material recovery
  • Refining and reintegration into U.S. supply chains

02 · International

Primary gold and silver production. A broader mineral endowment.

Our international mining operations produce gold and silver as primary products, processed and refined within the APEX value chain. The same mineralized system hosts a broader suite of critical minerals, which we allocate to qualified U.S. refining and off-take partners. Our field record documents surface excavation and underground workings, and our technical review covers gold-focused exploration across the licensed area.

  • Primary production processed within the APEX value chain
  • Geological, alteration and aeromagnetic review
  • Export license in place for U.S.-bound supply
What we bring

The foundation, the access, the license.

01 · The foundation

Established operations at home and abroad: electronics processing and refining in the United States, and mining internationally.
