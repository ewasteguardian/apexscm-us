What we do, and where.

01 · In the United States

Electronics: collected, processed, refined.

We are an established operator in the collection, secure processing and refining of end-of-life electronics. Our lifecycle model connects data destruction and refurbishment with material recovery and reintegration, under chain-of-custody control and documented outcomes.

  • Secure collection and certified data destruction
  • Processing, refurbishment and material recovery
  • Refining and reintegration into U.S. supply chains

02 · In the Philippines

Mining gold and silver, with more in the ground.

We mine gold and silver for ourselves. The same workings carry a wider set of critical minerals that we are open to supplying onward. Our field record documents surface excavation and underground workings, and our technical review covers gold-focused exploration north of Masara in Mindanao.

  • Own mining operations and field program
  • Geological, alteration and aeromagnetic review
  • Export license in place for U.S.-bound supply
What we bring

The foundation, the access, the license.

01 · The foundation

Established operations on both sides of the Pacific: electronics processing and refining in the USA, mining in the Philippines.

02 · The access

Our own mine, producing gold and silver alongside tungsten, copper, uranium, cobalt, antimony, zinc, lead and iron.

03 · The license

An export license in hand, so material can move from the Philippines to refineries in the United States.

A U.S. refinery or off-taker that needs a dependable, licensed source of critical minerals.
